02 · What to include

A few specifics turn a first email into a useful answer. None of it is required — send what you have.

  • +Target device. Vendor, family and part, if it is chosen.
  • +Image format. Resolution, bit depth and number of bands.
  • +Throughput. Frame rate or pixel rate you have to hit.
  • +Constraints. Resource budget, clock target, and anything else the design has to fit inside.
  • +Stage and timeline. Study, prototype or flight build — and when you need it.
