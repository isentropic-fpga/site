IP cores & services

Standards-compliant IP for on-board compression, and FPGA design & verification services for the imaging and aerospace industries.

01 · IP core

A JPEG-LS encoder IP core for FPGA — real-time, lossless image compression at a fraction of the computational cost of JPEG 2000 lossless, with no external memory. Single-component, one pixel per clock, and vendor-agnostic, so it targets any platform and scales across sensor bands.

Conformance is proven byte-exact against CharLS, an independent open-source implementation of ISO/IEC 14495-1. It is dual-licensed — free under GPL v3, with a commercial license for proprietary products.
